# Editing the Broadford Cemetery Trust website

The website's words, pictures, fees are edited at the content editor's address with your own email address and password. Nothing you do there changes the website until you press Publish, so you can take your time.

## Signing in

Open the editor's address in any browser. Type your email address, then your password, then press Sign in. If you have forgotten the password, ask the person who manages the editor for a new invitation link; the editor does not send email itself. The first screen lists the websites you may edit. Choose Broadford Cemetery Trust.

## Finding what you want to change

The site's screen has two lists. Pages and settings holds one item per page of the website (Home page, About page, Cemetery fees page, and so on) plus two items which appear on every page: Site details, with the addresses, email address, footer text, the banner across the top; Menu, with the links in the navigation. Lists holds content which repeats: the trustees, the photographs of the grounds, the fee tables, the history timeline, the sections of the Policies page. If a change belongs to one page, start with that page; if it is a person, a photograph, a fee or a dated event, start with the list.

Every item has a short description under its name saying what it covers. Fields inside have a plain label, with a sentence underneath where it helps, saying how the text is used. A red asterisk marks a field the page cannot do without.

## Changing text

Most fields are a single line or a short box. The larger boxes with a row of buttons above them (Bold, Italic, Link, Heading, Bullets, Quote) hold paragraphs of text. Select a word and press Link to attach a web address; press Link again with the cursor on a link to change or remove it. Use Heading for a heading within the text and Sub-heading for one beneath it. Plain removes formatting from the selection.

The editor saves your work every few seconds as you type. The status in the panel on the right says Unsaved changes, then Unpublished changes with the time it was saved. If you close the browser, the work is still there when you come back, marked in the lists as Unpublished changes with your name.

## Pictures

A picture field shows the current picture with two buttons. Change picture opens the pictures the website already has. Choose one, or press Upload a new picture to add a photograph from your computer. JPG, PNG or WebP files up to 8 MB are accepted. Large photographs straight from a phone or camera are often several megabytes; make them smaller first so pages load quickly for visitors. Every picture has a description field beside it for people using screen readers: say what the photograph shows in a sentence.

The Pictures link at the top of the site's screen shows everything uploaded so far.

## Lists of things

A trustee, a fee or a timeline entry is one item in its list. Press Add one to make a new item, give it a name, then fill in its fields. Up or Down changes the order where the order matters on the page; Remove deletes an item (it stays in the earlier versions and can be brought back).

Fee tables are grouped by section (Right of interment; Burial and interment; Memorials and permits; Records and certificates). Open a section to change its tables and the rows within them. Amounts are in dollars; leave the amount empty and fill in the wording field for a fee which is quoted on request.

## Seeing the page before publishing

Preview the page builds a private copy of the website with your unpublished changes and opens it in a new tab, with a brown strip along the bottom to remind you it is a preview. The first preview after a while takes about a minute; later ones a few seconds. Nothing in the preview is visible to the public.

## Publishing

Press Publish to the website, then Yes, publish. The change is recorded with your name. The website is rebuilt, which takes a minute or two. The site's screen shows whether the rebuild is still running or has finished. Throw away changes drops what you have done since the last publish and goes back to what is on the website.

## Undoing a publish

Every publish is kept. Earlier versions lists each one with its date, with who made it. Choose a version to see its text, with the lines which differ from the current version marked, and press Bring this version back to publish it again. What has changed, on the site's screen, lists every publish, upload or removal made through the editor.

## Things to know

Two people can work on the site at once, but one unpublished change per item is kept; if someone else has saved a draft of the same item you will see their name with the time at the top. Talk to them before publishing over it.

The banner at the top of every page, which says the site is a proof of concept, is the first field under Site details. Clear it when the Trust is ready for the site to be seen.

## Grave records

Plots, burials, rights of interment, requests from the public and stories live under Cemetery records, the first button on the site's screen. Changes there are saved straight away, with your name in the Change log, but reach the website only when someone presses Publish to the website on the Overview page.

The Map shows every plot over an aerial photo, coloured by status. Search a surname or a plot reference such as CE-5-7 and the map moves to it. Open the plot to change its details, add photos or add a person. Edit outlines, under the map, reshapes or moves a plot, or draws a new one.

Requests sent from the website arrive under Requests with a reference such as R-4KX9TQ, which the sender also sees. Give a request a date and it appears on the Calendar. An approved plot purchase can become a right of interment with one button; an approved burial or placement can become a burial record the same way.

Stories sent from the website wait under Stories until someone approves them. Private details never reach the website: causes of death, private notes, contacts, rights of interment, payments and requests stay in the editor.

Reports and exports downloads any of the records as a spreadsheet. Downloads holding private details are recorded in the Change log.
